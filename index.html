You’ve built a good business. So why is growth getting harder?

Lead flow swings with the season.

Busy for six weeks, then quiet. Referrals and repeat work carry more of the load than you’d like, and you can’t forecast the next quarter with confidence.

The right jobs go to someone else.

A competitor with a sharper offer, a faster reply or a stronger reputation wins the quote, and often it’s the work you wanted most.

Everything still runs through you.

Quotes, calls, decisions, problems. Each new job adds to your plate rather than lifting it, and the business can only grow as fast as you can.
